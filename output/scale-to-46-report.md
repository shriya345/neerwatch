Tiles are grouped by date, platform, relative orbit and processing baseline, with at most 300 seconds difference in provider STAC timestamps. Each source is scaled using its own native scale/offset, reprojected to the shared 60 m UTM grid, and assigned a deterministic integer owner per grid pixel. All spectral bands use the same owner; overlap pixels are not averaged or counted twice. QC is applied after mosaicking, including unchanged 95% full-reservoir footprint and all cloud/water/shoreline/reflectance gates. No observations from different days are combined. Composite identifiers are explicitly distinguished from source scene IDs, which remain saved in source_scenes.

## Scientific audit

Equation: (35.75 × B05/B04 − 19.30)^1.124. Coefficients were not changed to increase coverage. [Gilerson et al.](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.024109) and the existing FCMm reference expression were used for numerical agreement checks. NDCI remains dimensionless. Native DN scale/offset are retained per source band; all calculation inputs are reflectance. Output mg/m³ and µg/L are numerically equivalent. The provider Sen2Cor BOA approximation is experimental and is not ACOLITE aquatic correction. See data/internal/scale-algorithm-audit.json.

EPA categories remain ≤2 oligotrophic, >2–7 mesotrophic, >7–30 eutrophic and >30 hypereutrophic µg/L. They describe trophic screening and are not bloom probability or toxicity alerts. Source: https://nationallakesassessment.epa.gov/webreport/#trophic-state.

## Remaining limitations

Satellite-derived chlorophyll-a estimates have not yet been validated against Karnataka field measurements. Full water-specific atmospheric correction, local calibration, toxicity detection and forecasting are not provided. No field accuracy scores were manufactured. Browser visual alignment/layout verification remains incomplete. The running app is local at http://127.0.0.1:8010; no external production deployment was changed. Approximately 140 reservoirs remains a future architectural goal, not an asserted authoritative denominator or this phase’s publication target.
